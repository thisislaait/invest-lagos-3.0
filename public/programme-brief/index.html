This document sets out the confirmed programme speakers for the Lagos State Investment Forum 2026, the information we require from each speaker's team ahead of the event, and the sessions where speaker confirmation remains outstanding.

We ask the Committee to review the pending items in Section C and to share the relevant speaker management files with the Conference Content Team at the earliest opportunity. A consolidated speaker information file — including official biographies, headshots, and confirmed designations — will allow us to complete all production materials on schedule.
Section A
Information Required from All Speaker Teams

The following items are required for every confirmed speaker on the programme, without exception. Where a speaker's team has already submitted this information, it is noted against their entry in Section B.

  • 01Professional headshot — High-resolution photograph, minimum 2MB, portrait orientation, plain or neutral background preferred. JPEG or PNG format.
  • 02Official biography — 150 to 200 words, written in the third person, reflecting the speaker's current role and relevant achievements.
  • 03Confirmed name, title, and institutional affiliation — Exactly as it should appear on screen and in the official programme. Any changes to title or designation must be communicated no later than 4 June.
  • 04Presentation or slide deck — Where applicable. PowerPoint or PDF format, 16:9 (widescreen). Due no later than 5 June 2026 to allow technical review.
  • 05Confirmed arrival time and any protocol requirements — Required for all Ministers, Governors, Heads of Government, and other VIP principals. To be coordinated through the Protocol team.
