Conference Programme
Main Plenary Hall + Breakout A & B - Eko Hotel & Suites, Victoria Island, Lagos
Scene 01
07:00 – 07:30
Hotel Exterior & Approach
Eko Hotel Entrance, Adetokunbo Ademola Street, Victoria Island
Delegates arriving by car or bus see the Forum's first visual signals before they step out - branding at all key eye-level positions on the hotel façade, directional signage from the drop-off zone, security presence established and visible. The perimeter is set. The message lands before anyone walks through a door: this is a major event and it is organised. First impression starts at the gate, not the lobby.
ScreensLarge format outdoor screens or LED displays at the hotel entrance running the LSIF brand film on loop - Lagos aerial footage, infrastructure, the Forum logo. No text-heavy slides. Atmosphere, not information. Outdoor banners and vinyl: LSIF 2026 / Lagos State Government / CWEIC co-branding at eye level on the façade.
AudioOutdoor PA at low volume: smooth instrumental African jazz (Ebo Taylor, Hugh Masekela influenced). Audible enough to signal an event is happening, low enough that arriving delegates can speak without competing with it.
ProtocolDSS and hotel security perimeter is established by 06:30. Dedicated VVIP drop-off lane separate from general hotel traffic. Obehi coordinates with protocol officers at the entrance to receive and direct principals. Presidential advance sweep completed before delegates begin arriving.
StaffGbemi coordinates with ushers in position at the entrance in navy and gold - welcoming, directing, no standing with arms folded. Signage posts run from drop-off → entrance → registration hall. Nobody should be unsure where to go.
Delegates enter the building

Eko Hotel Conference Foyer / Pre-Function Area

Equilibrist Act - Chair Balance & Stacking
Grand Ballroom - Main Stage / Performance Floor
The MC says nothing. Lights dim. A single spotlight hits the stage. A performer walks out carrying a chair. By the time the act ends, they are balanced on a tower of stacked chairs several metres high, in complete stillness. The room is completely silent. Some guests will have risen slightly from their seats without realising. This is the moment that gets described at breakfast tomorrow. It requires precision from Alhassan and Damilare's technical team - the staging of this act is as important as the act itself.
AV CueCritical lighting sequence: house lights drop to 20% as the performer enters. Single tight spotlight follows throughout. Ceiling clearance at Eko Hotel ballroom must be measured and confirmed at the production recce - the full stacked height of the act must clear the ceiling with margin. Screen cameras track the performance live so delegates at the back see the scale. Complete silence from house PA during the act.
AudioZero PA during the performance. The only sound is the performer's movement and the audience's collective held breath. After the final balance is held: 3 full seconds of silence before the performer descends - do not rush to the music cue. When they descend safely: 10-second dramatic music swell from PA, then cut to natural applause.
StaffAll floor waiting staff hold from 20:35 - zero movement during the act. Jacqui coordinates with the hospitality team, Lola coordinates with F&B service. Ugo briefs all floor staff before this slot. No exceptions. Service resumes only when applause begins.
